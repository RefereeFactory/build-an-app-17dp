How this app works

  1. Browse treatments and see the current hourly rate, set by the therapist.
  2. Check your area — enter a postcode and we use The Factory's places service to measure the distance from our base, confirm you're inside the travel area, and estimate any travel contribution.
  3. Request a booking — your details are stored securely so we can confirm and keep in touch (only if you tick consent).
  4. Pay safely — payments run through The Factory's secure facility; we never see card details. After paying you'll return here and your session is confirmed with us directly.
  5. Owner area — the therapist sets prices, location and travel area, and can export consented contacts as a CSV for follow-ups.
